A compact Dynamic-Island-style entry point at the top center of the screen that reuses GNOME Shell's own clock, calendar and notifications. It takes the place of the top bar clock, which it makes transparent while the pill is shown (a setting turns this off).

Click the pill, rest the pointer on it, or press the shortcut to open it. Its tabs:
- Clock: weekday, time and date.
- Calendar: the events of the calendars GNOME already knows (Online Accounts, GNOME Calendar, Evolution), read-only, through Evolution Data Server. It needs the gir1.2-ecal-2.0 package (Debian and Ubuntu); without it the tab says what to install.
- Notifications: GNOME's own notifications, with Do Not Disturb. Froonty keeps no copy.
- Notes: Markdown notes, kept as plain files in a folder you choose (~/.local/share/froonty/notes by default), with labels and a window to search them all.
The top row also offers panic buttons that mute the microphone or the sound, and GNOME's own calendar and notification menu.

Froonty makes no network requests of its own. On a click, the Calendar tab opens an event's day in the web calendar it comes from (in your browser), or GNOME Settings' Online Accounts.
